## About HDFC Flexi Cap Fund

An open ended dynamic equity scheme investing across large cap, mid cap & small cap stocks
30+ Years of Growth & Trust
Since inception, HDFC Flexi Cap Fund has navigated multiple market cycle over more than 3 decades
Flexi Cap Funds offer fund managers complete flexibility to allocate assets across large, mid, and small-cap stocks, based on market conditions. This adaptability makes HDFC Flexi Cap Fund attractive to investors seeking diversified exposure to the equity market.

## What is HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?

Fund Description: HDFC Flexi Cap Fund is an open-ended equity mutual fund scheme that invests across large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap companies.
This allows the fund to combine:
Stability from established market leaders
Growth potential from mid-sized companies
Emerging opportunities from smaller businesses
helping create a balanced approach to long-term wealth creation.

## Who can consider HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?

This fund may suit investors who:
Have a long-term investment horizon (5+ years)
Want diversified equity exposure across market caps
Are comfortable with short-term market volatility for long-term growth
